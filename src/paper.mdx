MOO (Multi-view Oriented Observations) is a synthetic dataset for cattle re-identification. It comprises 128,000 images of 1,000 individuals. Designed for systematic viewpoint analysis in AG-ReID, each image is annotated with its azimuth and elevation, camera calibration data, and depth maps. MOO enables rigorous study of the impact of viewpoint variations on identification performance, and serves as an effective pre-training source for transfer to real-world data.

MO0 is publicly available at [https://github.com/TurtleSmoke/MOO](https://github.com/TurtleSmoke/MOO).
